## Why a green proof is not available here

1. **Minimality** among all `Monotone2` circuits for `graphFun G ∅` requires a global argument (Nat.find over existence is easy; proving the witness is **the** minimum needs comparing all competitors). The fleet’s intended route is exhaustive search on small graphs plus an exchange argument — neither is formalized as a lemma importable from Defs alone.

2. **HasStarProfile** on that **same** minimum circuit is strictly stronger than “build per-edge AND + OR tree” (which has star profile but is usually **not** minimal). The reduction’s step 2 (`h2`) assumes a minimal star-profile circuit and repairs toward `Owns`; step 1 must supply that object — it is not discharged by cover circuits or biclique-block cost.

3. **No counterexample** was found in-repo; the statement aligns with the L-meta-1031 conjecture. Blocker is **missing formal lemmas** (exchange, small-graph certificates, or a definitional bridge from TopForm / least-AO to `Monotone2` + `HasStarProfile`), not `statement_seems_false`.
